- Graceful Pod deletion lets the active SSE finish.
- Direct JVM `SIGKILL` may cut the socket, but a reconnect through another Pod completes the same durable turn.
- Session history remains present and Aurora bubbles are committed exactly once.

- The first run used the former fixed two-minute generation lease and completed in `121.365s`; this directly motivated a configurable lease.

- A reconnect through the surviving Service returned `replay.completed` / `COMPLETED` in `1.546s`.
